📘 Simple, Fast, and Reliable

MyHAC Companion connects securely to your school district’s Home Access Center (HAC) so you can:

  • View grades and class averages instantly
  • Track missing or upcoming assignments
  • Check attendance and progress reports
  • Enjoy a clean, distraction-free interface designed for parents and students alike

No login delays, no unnecessary taps — open the app and see what matters most.

🔒 Your Data Stays Yours

Your login credentials are stored only on your iPhone, protected by Apple Keychain encryption. MyHAC Companion never sends, shares, or stores your information on any external server.

It simply refreshes your data each time you launch the app — nothing runs in the background.

❤️ Designed by a Parent, for Parents

Built with simplicity in mind — no ads, no tracking, no subscription fees. Just a fast, free app that makes checking grades easy for busy families.
